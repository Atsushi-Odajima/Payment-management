The two files in this directory are subsets of Noto Sans JP v2.004 produced
with fontTools' pyftsubset (see scripts/build-pdf-font.mjs):

  NotoSansJP-Regular.ttf  JIS X 0208 (kana, symbols, JIS level 1 + 2 kanji)
  NotoSansJP-Bold.ttf     JIS X 0208 without level 2 kanji

Hinting and OpenType layout tables were dropped to keep the PDF functions
small; at render time each document is subset again to the characters it
uses (HarfBuzz via subset-font) before pdf-lib embeds it.
